## Frequently Asked Questions
To earn the maximum yuu Points, when shopping at yuu merchants, you will need to perform these two steps at checkout:
1.Scan your yuu ID/ link your yuu ID when transacting instore/ online, and
2.Pay with your DBS yuu Card
To redeem cash rebates:
You can use your yuu Points to offset purchases and redeem instantly at merchants like Cold Storage, Giant, Guardian, 7-Eleven and foodpanda.
To redeem miles:
You can redeem miles from the yuu App. Simply log in to your yuu App > Me > Transfer to Krisflyer to transfer your yuu Points to miles instantly.
To redeem cash vouchers or offers:
You can redeem cash vouchers or offers from the yuu App. Simply log in to your yuu App > Discover to browse all redemption offers
You can earn 5% cash rebates or 2.8 miles per S$1 with no minimum spend. To qualify for the additional 13% cash rebates or 7.2 miles per S$1 each calendar month, you must:
Spend a minimum of S$800 eligible card spend, and
Transact at 4 different Participating Merchants (including SimplyGo).
If you did not meet the Qualifying Spend Criteria for Bonus Rewards (with min. spend), you will get 5% cash rebates or 2.8 miles per S$1 on Participating Merchants, capped at S$144 or 28,800 miles.
