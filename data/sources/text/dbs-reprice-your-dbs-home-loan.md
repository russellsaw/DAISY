## Reprice your existing DBS home loan
Repricing your DBS Home Loan means switching to a new loan package with us. You may enjoy potential mortgage savings with a new loan package.
## Why reprice with DBS?
Market conditions may turn in your favour so you may enjoy a lower interest rate and reduced mortgage repayments when you reprice your DBS Home Loan. This gives you the flexibility to assess your financial goals and customise your home loan package according to your budget and needs.
## Option to revise outstanding loan tenure
You have the option to revise your outstanding loan tenure -- subjected to approval and fulfilment of regulatory guidelines.

## Process to reprice your loan
1Check your home loan commitment expiry dateYou may view your home loan’s commitment period expiry by clicking into your home loan account on digibank. We recommend exploring repricing options with us 3-4 months before your current loan's lock-in period ends.
2Submit your repricing application onlineChoose a loan package that suits your needs and submit your application in minutes. If we require more information, our home loan specialist will reach out to you.
3Total Debt Servicing Ratio (TDSR) assessmentThis applies to investment properties, properties with mortgage withdrawal loans and commercial properties. Please submit your income documents for assessment.
4Receive letter of variationOnce your loan is approved, you will receive a letter of variation via email for your acceptance.
5Accept loan offerOnce you accept the loan offer, we will email you the letter of acceptance. Your revised rate package will take effect on the date stated in the letter.
