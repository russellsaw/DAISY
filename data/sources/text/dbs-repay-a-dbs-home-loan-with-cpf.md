## Repay home loan partially / fully using CPF funds
Learn how you can use CPF funds to partially or fully repay your home loan.
For home loan full repayment request
Visit CPF Website and login with your Singpass.
Under Home ownership, select Using CPF for housing.
Under Make a full or partial housing loan payment, select Make loan payment.
Read through Important notes and select the checkbox to acknowledge then click Start.
Ensure property address is correct and select Make a full housing loan payment then Housing loan.
Complete the following then click Next:
Bank: DBS Bank Ltd
Principal outstanding: Key in your outstanding loan balance as seen on digibank
Accrued interest: You may leave this blank
Amount to be deducted from CPF: Key in repayment amount from your CPF account
Date of deduction: Indicate the deduction date as 3 working days before the payment date of your full repayment request
Full redemption statement: Please upload the full redemption statement that DBS will mail to you within 14 working days of receiving your full redemption request.
Review your application details and select the checkbox to acknowledge that you accept the Terms and Conditions, then click Submit.
For home loan partial repayment request
Visit CPF Website and login with your Singpass.
Under Home ownership, select Using CPF for housing.
Under Make a full or partial housing loan payment, select Make loan payment.
Read through Important notes and select the checkbox to acknowledge then click Start.
Ensure property address is correct and select Make a partial housing loan payment.
Complete the following then click Next:
Bank: DBS Bank Ltd
Amount to be deducted from CPF: Key in repayment amount from your CPF account
Date of deduction: Indicate the deduction date as 3 working days before the payment date of your partial repayment request.
Review your application details and select the checkbox to acknowledge that you accept the Terms and Conditions, then click Submit.
