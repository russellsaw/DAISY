14. “Minimum Spend Requirement ” is defined as retail transaction(s) charged to the DBS Chromo Card
(“Card”) and computed after deducting any transaction cancelled, voided, refunded or reversed . This
includes transactions made in foreign currencies, as well as transactions made in foreign currencies and
converted to Singapore Dollars via the dynamic currency conversion (“DCC”). Transactions excluded from
the Minimum Spend Requirement include posted 0% interest free instalment plan monthly transactions
(“IPP”), posted My Preferred Payment instalment plan monthly transactions (“MP3”), interest, finance
charges, cash advance, cash withdrawal, balance transfer, smart cash, AXS payments (except Pay+Earn),
SAM online bill payments, bill payments via Internet banking, all fees charged by DBS and any transaction
that is subsequently cancelled, voided, refunded or reversed (“Refunded Transactions”) for any reason.
Refunded Transactions will be deducted from the Minimum Spend Requirement, when computing any
cashback or miles.
Rewards Preference: Cashback
15. Cardmembers will earn 1% cashback (“Base cashback”) on all Eligible Spend charged to Card. Any
cancelled, voided, refunded, or reversed transaction will be deducted from Eligible Spend and Minimum
Spend Requirement.
16. To qualify for the additional 4% cashback (“Bonus cashback”) on the top two (2) Spend Categories,
Cardmembers must charge at least S$500 worth of retail transaction(s) that count towards the Minimum
Spend Requirement to the Card in the same calendar month.
17. Bonus cashback is capped at S$30 per Spend Category per calendar month. The maximum cashback a
Cardmember can earn is capped at S$150 per calendar month. This cap includes both Base cashback and
Bonus cashback earned in the same calendar month.”
18. In the event two (2) or more Spend Categories have the same aggregate Eligible Spend in a calendar month,
DBS shall determine, at its sole and absolute discretion, which Spend Categories shall qualify for Bonus
cashback.
19. Transactions made in foreign currencies will not qualify for Base cashback or Bonus cashback.
20. For the avoidance of doubt, transactions made overseas and converted to Singapore Dollars via DCC will be
considered as Eligible Spend and will qualify for Base cashback and Bonus cashback, in accordance with
Table 1.
21. Cashback earned will be posted in the monthly Card Account statement.
22. Cashback will not be awarded to a Cardmember whose Card Account is delinquent, voluntarily, or
involuntarily closed, terminated, or suspended for any reasons whatsoever before the cashback is awarded
into the Card Account.

Rewards Preference: Miles
23. Cardmembers who switch to miles Rewards Preference will earn DBS Points equivalent to 1.3 miles per S$1
spent on Eligible Spend charged to their Card.
24. Transactions made in foreign currencies will not qualify for miles.
25. For the avoidance of doubt, transactions made overseas and converted to Singapore Dollars DCC will be
considered as Eligible Spend and will qualify for miles, in accordance with Table 1.
26. 1.3 Miles will be awarded in the form of DBS Points (1 DBS Point equivalent to 2 miles) and is calculated
based on the total transaction amount divided by S$5 and multiplied by 3.25X DBS Point, rounded down to
the nearest whole number and be credited to Cardmember’s account within 30 days following the end of that
calendar month.
27. DBS Points earned on DBS Chromo Card will have no expiry.
28. The Cardmember must ensure that their Card Account is in good standing with DBS, not terminated and/or
suspended in order to perform a rewards redemption.
29. DBS Points may only be redeemed or used in accordance with the DBS Rewards Programme Terms and
Conditions. Please refer to the full DBS Rewards Programme Terms and Conditions for the complete list of
eligible redemption options and applicable conditions.
Switching Rewards Preference
30. The rewards switching functionality is available only via the DBS digibank app. To make the switch,
Cardmembers must first download the DBS digibank app from the App Store, Google Play or
AppGallery, then sign in using their DBS digibank account.
31. Cardmembers may switch their Rewards Preference only once per calendar month. If a Cardmember has
made a switch in the current calendar month, the next switch may only be made in the following calendar
month. All Reward Preference switches are final, irreversible, and not subject to dispute.
32. Cardmembers may decide or switch their Rewards Preference for the previous month’s Eligible Spend by
the 10 th day of the following calendar month. The Rewards Preference in effect on the 10 th day of the
month will apply to that previous month’s Eligible Spend. The Rewards Preference selected by the
Cardmember at the reward computation date determined by DBS shall apply to the computation of
cashback and/or DBS Points for the corresponding calendar month’s Eligible Spend.
a. For example, if a Cardmember has a Eligible Spend in April, the Cardmember may decide or switch
their Rewards Preference by 10 May. The Rewards Preference in effect on 10 May will determine if
the Cardmember receives cashback or miles for their Eligible Spend in April.
b. If a Cardmember has Eligible Spend in May and is on miles Rewards Preference on 10 June, the
Cardmember will receive DBS Points for all Eligible Spend in May.
33. Base cashback and/or DBS Points earned on Eligible Spend charged in a calendar month will be credited to
the Cardmember’s account within 30 days following the end of that calendar month.

Card Design Change
42. New Cardmembers can change their card design from the next calendar month following the month the Card
Account is successfully created.
43. Cardmembers may change card design once per calendar month via the DBS digibank app. If Cardmembers
have made one change within a calendar month, they will be eligible to make another card design change
only in the next calendar month.
44. Each card design change may incur a fee, subjected to GST, where applicable, which will be advised to the
Cardmember during the card design selection journey. The fee will be charged to Cardmember’s account
upon successful processing of the design change request. Any fees charged are irreversible, and not subject
to dispute once the request has been successful processed.
Other Rewards Conditions
45. Minimum Spend Requirement and rewards are computed based on transaction date charged to the Card
after deducting any transaction cancelled, voided, refunded or reversed , provided that such transaction(s)
have been posted into the Card Account as at the point of computation of the rewards.
46. Cashback earned for each transaction is rounded down to the nearest 2 decimal places.
47. DBS shall not be responsible for any failure or delay in the posting of sales transaction(s), which may result
in any Cardmember being omitted from enjoying the benefits of the Chromo Card Programme.
48. DBS reserves the right to determine the applicable Rewards Preference, and such determination shall be
final.
49. DBS reserves the right at any time, without giving any reason or notice to Cardmembers, to deduct,
withdraw, or cancel any Rewards credited to them without liability. Cardmembers will not be entitled to
any payment or compensation whatsoever in respect of such deduction, withdrawal, or cancellation.
50. DBS’ decision on all matters relating to the Chromo Card Programme shall be final. No correspondence or
claims will be entertained.
