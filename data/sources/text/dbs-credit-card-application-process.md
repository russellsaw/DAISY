## Apply for a Credit Card
Interested in getting a DBS or POSB Credit Card? Simply submit your application via DBS website or digibank.
## Important information
For your security, do not choose an easily identifiable PIN such as User ID, contact number, birthdate, repeated or consecutive numbers.
Your application will be reviewed within 15 working days. We will contact you via email if we need more information.
You will receive an email notification once your application has been processed.
Learn more about Documents Required for Card Application.
## How to apply for a Credit Card
There are various channels which you may apply for a Credit Card with us. The most convenient method would be via digibank mobile.
digibank mobile
1
Step 1
Log in to digibank mobile with your Touch / Face ID or digibank User ID & PIN.
2
Step 2
Tap on More at the bottom and under Apply, select Cards.
3
Step 3
Tap on Credit Cards.
4
Step 4
Select card that you wish to apply for and tap Apply.
5
Step 5
Check your personal details, enter your Preferred name to be printed on card and indicate the Promo Code(if any). Tap Next to proceed.
6
Step 6
Review your application details and tap Next.
7
Step 7
Create your Card PIN and tap Submit to complete your application.
Get the latest DBS digibank mobile app now!
digibank Online
1
Step 1
Log in to digibank Online with your User ID & PIN.
2
Step 2
Complete the Authentication Process.
3
Step 3
Click on Apply on the left menu and under Cards, select Credit Cards.
4
Step 4
Click Apply now on the Card you wish to apply and click Proceed.
5
Step 5
Read through the features of your preferred card and click Start.
6
Step 6
Check your details and enter your Preferred name to appear on card, Promo Code(if any). Tap Next.
7
Step 7
Review your application details and click Next.
8
Step 8
Create aPIN for your new card and click Submit to complete your application.
9
Step 9
Your application is submitted successfully.

1

Step 2

3
Step 3

4
Step 4
After filling up your Application Details and click Continue.
5
Step 5
Verify your Application Details and click Continue if your information is correct.
6
Step 6
Create your Card PIN and click Submit to complete your credit card application.
