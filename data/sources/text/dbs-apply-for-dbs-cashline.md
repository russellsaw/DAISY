## Apply for DBS Cashline
If you are new to DBS/POSB Bank, you can apply for DBS Cashline with us online. For existing customers, application can be made easily via digibank.
## How to apply for DBS Cashline
There are various channels which you may apply for DBS Cashline with us. The most convenient method would be via digibank mobile.
digibank mobile
1
Step 1
Log in to digibank mobile with your Touch / Face ID or digibank User ID & PIN.
2
Step 2
Tap on Apply at the bottom. Under Borrow, tap on Personal Loans.
3
Step 3
Scroll down to Cashline and tap on Apply now.
4
Step 4
Tap Start.
5
Step 5
Check that your Contact details, Address details and Personal details are up to date. Then, tap Next.
6
Step 6
Enter your Employment details, Company address and select your preferred Cashline limit. Tap Next.
7
Step 7
Verify your application details and click Confirm to complete your application.
Get the latest DBS digibank mobile app now!
digibank Online
If you are an existing customer with DBS/POSB Bank, you may apply for a DBS Cashline Account via digibank Online.
1
Step 1
Log in to digibank Online with your User ID & PIN.
2
Step 2
Complete the Authentication Process.
3
Step 3
Click Apply on the left menu and under Borrow, click on Personal Loans.
4
Step 4
Under Cashline, click on Apply now.
5
Step 5
Read through the information and click Start.
6
Step 6
Verify your Contact and Address details, enter your Personal details and click Next.
7
Step 7
Enter your Employment details, Company address and select your preferred Cashline limit. Click Next.
8
Step 8
Verify your application details and click Submit to complete your application.
9
Step 9
You have submitted your DBS Cashline application successfully.
