## Turning 30 soon?
Wondering what happens to your card once you turn 30? We’ve got you covered!
You will continue to earn up to 5% cashback or 1.3 miles per S$1 spend for 3 months after your 30th birthday month.
Here are some changes you can expect in the 4th month after your birthday:
If you opt for cashback rewards, you will earn 1.7% cashback on your eligible local spend, with no cap and no minimum spend requirement.
If you opt for miles rewards, you will earn 1.3 miles per S$1 spend (awarded in the form of DBS Points) on eligible local spend, with no cap and no minimum spend requirement.
You will continue to enjoy the other benefits, including 0 FX fees, the flexibility to toggle reward preference between cashback or miles, and the ability to change your card design at a fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions
The DBS Chromo Card is a cashback or miles credit card made exclusively for young adults aged 21 to 29.
The DBS Chromo Card rewards you with up to 5% cashback on your top 2 local spend categories every month, 0 FX fees, a 5-year annual fee waiver and the ability to toggle between cashback or miles.
The 2 local spend categories with the highest eligible spend in each calendar month will automatically qualify for Bonus cashback and you will not be required to select your categories.
You will need to meet a minimum spend requirement of S$500 per calendar month to earn up to 5% cashback on your top 2 local spend categories in the same month.
The maximum total cashback awarded per calendar month is S$150. This cap includes both Base cashback and Bonus cashback earned in the same calendar month. Within this overall limit, Bonus cashback for each of the top 2 local spend categories is capped at S$30 per calendar month.
