## How to apply for Supplementary Credit Card
Principal Cardholder invites Supplementary Card Applicant via digibank app. Applicant accepts invite instantly in their own digibank app.
Principal CardholderSupplementary Card Applicant
After logging into digibank, tap on Apply in the bottom navigation bar.
Under the Cards section, tap on Credit Cards then Supplementary Credit Card tab.
Read the important notes, select the credit card that you wish to apply for, and enter all the required details.
Ensure Supplementary Card Applicant’s info are correct and tap Submit.
Please remind your Supplementary Card Applicant to accept the invite.
After logging into digibank, tap on More in the bottom navigation bar.
Under the Apply section, tap on Cards and select Resume your saved application.
Enter all the required Supplementary Card Applicant’s info.
Enter and confirm your new card PIN, and tap Submit.
Your card will be delivered to your Principal cardholder within 5 working days for Singapore addresses and at least 14 working days for overseas addresses.
