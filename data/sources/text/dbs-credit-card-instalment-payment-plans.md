## Frequently Asked Questions
There is no interest fee when you use DBS Instalment Payment Plan. Your purchase amount is simply split into equal monthly instalments, with no additional interest charged.
Minimum purchase amounts, specific terms and conditions and products for instalment plan may vary for different merchants.
In-Store: Ask for DBS Instalment Payment Plan when you are making payment at any of the participating merchant outlets here
Online: Select DBS Instalment Payment Plans and preferred tenure at check out.
Purchases made through DBS Instalment Payment Plan will not earn DBS Rewards Points or Daily$.
There is no maximum plans that one can hold, do note that the cap will be based on your available credit limit. Once the credit limit is used up, no new plan can be created.
You may terminate the instalment payment plan by contacting our Customer Care Hotline. However, do note that S$150 termination fee is applicable for each plan termination and its non-waivable. Once plan is terminated, outstanding balance of the plan will be made payable immediately.
