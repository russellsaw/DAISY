## Using CPF for monthly repayment
Learn how to commence or revise your CPF monthly repayment amount through CPF website using your Singpass.
## How to repay monthly home loan instalments using CPF funds
To commence/revise CPF monthly repayment
Visit CPF Website and login with your Singpass.
Under Home ownership, select Using CPF for housing.
Under Manage housing monthly instalments, select Manage instalments.
Select your property under Property Details and click Next.
Select Commence Monthly Instalment and click Next.
Read through the information before you click on Start and complete the following:
Financier: DBS Bank Ltd
Monthly Instalment: Indicate your monthly CPF instalment amount
Effective From: Select the month to commence the monthly instalment.
Read the Terms & Conditions and select the checkbox to provide your declaration, then click Next.
Review and confirm that the information you have provided is correct then click Submit.
Please note that you may also Revise Monthly Instalment or Cease Monthly Instalment following the steps above.
