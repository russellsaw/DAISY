Interest amortisation:
The amortisation of the amount of interest payment allocated to the monthly instalment is calculated using the Rule of 78 method (which is also known as the sum of digits method). Monthly interest and principal are apportioned differently; in the earlier months, a larger portion of your monthly repayment goes towards paying off the interest on the loan and gradually decreases over time.
