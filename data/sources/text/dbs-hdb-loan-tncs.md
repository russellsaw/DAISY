Terms & Conditions – DBS/POSB HDB Loan take-up with Complimentary LPA
Certification Promotion (“Promotion”)
1. This Promotion is valid from 15 July 2026 to 31 December 2026, with the DBS/POSB Home
Loan letter of offer dated by 31 December 2026 (“Promotion Period”)
2. This Promotion is valid for direct and indirect applications.
3. This Promotion is only valid for home loans granted by DBS/POSB to finance the purchase
of a HDB property, or to refinance an existing HDB loan obtained from another financial
institution or HDB (“HDB Home Loan”).
4. To qualify for this Promotion, customers must fulfil the conditions set out below within the
Promotion Period (“Eligible Customers”):
4.1. Loan application is submitted between 15 July 2026 and 31 December 2026, and whose
Letter of Offer is accepted between 15 July 2026 and 31 December 2026.
4.2. Complete conveyancing with one of the 3 participating law firms (“Our Partners”) as
follows:
• Apex Law (available for all HDB cases)
• Ascentsia Law (available for all HDB cases)
• PKWA Law (available for all HDB cases except for BTO and SBF)
5. Each borrower under a qualifying HDB Home Loan is entitled to one (1) complimentary
professional certification and witnessing of LPA Form 1, subject to a maximum of two (2)
complimentary certifications per qualifying loan.
5.1. This entitlement is strictly for the benefit of the borrower(s) of the qualifying loan only and
is not transferable to any other party.
5.2. In the case of joint borrowers for a single qualifying loan, each joint borrower is entitled
to one (1) complimentary LPA certification, up to a maximum of two (2) complimentary
LPA certifications per qualifying loan.
5.3. The complimentary LPA certification must be utilised by the borrower(s) for their own
LPA application(s) as a Donor.
6. Complimentary LPA Certification and Witnessing
6.1 Under this Promotion, the complimentary LPA certification and witnessing is provided
solely by participating partner law firms Apex Law, Ascentsia Law and PKWA Law.
6.2 The complimentary LPA certification and witnessing covers only the professional
certification and witnessing of the LPA Form 1 by Our Partners as the LPA Certificate
Issuer (CI).
6.3 The complimentary LPA certification offered under this Promotion is for LPA Form 1
for Singapore Citizens and Singapore Permanent Residents only and does not
include Form 2 LPAs, amendments, legal advisory work, or any other legal
documentation.
6.4 The complimentary LPA certification cannot be exchanged or converted into cash,
credit, rebates, or any other items.
6.5 The complimentary LPA certification must be completed with the Law Firm before loan
disbursement or within 3 months from loan acceptance date, whichever is earlier.
6.6 Government filing fees payable to the Office of the Public Guardian (OPG), if any, and
other third-party charges are not covered and must be borne by the customer. Any fee
waivers granted by OPG are administered solely by OPG and subject to their
prevailing terms.
6.7 This Campaign is not in any way related to, sponsored or endorsed by the Ministry of
Social and Family Development (“MSF”) or any of its programmes.
7. Customers must access the OPGO portal (Office of the Public Guardian Online) to
complete the online LPA application form, including the appointment and acceptance of
their donee(s), before visiting the law firm for certification. Any guidance prov ided by the law
firm on the online application is strictly at the law firm’s discretion and may vary by firm.

of any legal service provided by the partner law firms.
9. DBS/POSB is not incentivised by the partner law firms and does not receive any fees,
commissions, benefits or preferential arrangements arising from this offer. All legal services

arising from the LPA certification process or any other legal service performed by the

11. The value of the reward is considered a benefit received by you and will be deducted from

computation of the eligible loan amount.
12. Promotion is valid in conjunction with any other on-going offers, promotions, privileges, or
