## DBS Instant Loans Guide & Tips
How to choose the right Instant Loan: DBS Personal Loan vs DBS Cashline vs DBS Balance TransferTips to Streamline Your Loan Application Process
1Check loan eligibility for each instant loan optionVerify your bank loan eligibility status as a Singapore citizen, permanent resident, or foreigner.
2Compare interest rates using our calculator
3Assess your repayment needsChoose a fixed monthly repayment plan of up to 60 months with a Personal Loan or enjoy flexible repayment options with Cashline or Balance Transfer.
4Evaluate usage flexibility Choose a DBS Personal Loan for a fixed repayment tenures, Cashline for greater financial flexibility, and Balance Transfer for consolidating bills.
5Consider cashback benefitsFactor in attractive limited time offers: DBS Personal Loan's 3% unlimited cashback , Cashline's up to S$150 cashback , or Balance Transfer's 1% cashback!
1Prepare all required documents in advanceHave your NRIC (front and back), latest computerised payslip or salary crediting statement, and Income Tax Notice of Assessment ready before starting your loan application to avoid processing delays.
2Verify your loan eligibility beforehandEnsure you meet the minimum annual income of S$20,000, are within the required age range, or have an existing DBS Cashline/ Credit Card.
3Consider opening a DBS/POSB deposit account firstIf you don't have an existing account, set up your deposit account online before applying, as this is required for instant loan disbursement and can expedite your bank loan eligibility assessment.
