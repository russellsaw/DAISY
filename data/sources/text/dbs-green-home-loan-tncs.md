Terms and Conditions for DBS Green Home Loan Campaign (“Campaign”)
1. This Campaign is available from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026 (both dates
inclusive), unless otherwise extended or otherwise terminated by DBS in its sole
discretion (“Campaign Period”).
Eligibility
2. To qualify for the Campaign, the main applicant must successfully apply for a new DBS
Green Home Loan or refinance an existing home loan into a DBS Green Home Loan for a
residential property situated in Singapore during the Campaign Period.
3. The Green Home Loan is available for:
a. Private Building Under Construction (BUC) properties certified under the Building
and Construction Authority (BCA) Green Mark scheme; or
b. Selected private BUC properties earmarked for Green Mark certification
4. For properties certified under the BCA Green Mark scheme, the property must have
attained a BCA Green Mark rating as reflected on the SLEB Green Mark Building
Directory at point of loan application:
• Platinum Positive Energy
• Platinum Zero Energy
• Platinum Super Low Energy
• GoldPLUS Positive Energy
• GoldPLUS Zero Energy
• GoldPLUS Super Low Energy
• Positive Energy
• Zero Energy
• Super Low Energy
• Platinum
• GoldPLUS
5. The BCA Green Mark Rating must be valid at the point of application of the DBS Green
Home Loan for properties certified under BCA Green Mark scheme.
6. The selection of private BUC properties earmarked for Green Mark certification is
determined by DBS’ internal assessment of projects earmarked for Green Mark
certification.
