# DAISY against the enterprise banking guidelines

This page lists the rules and bank frameworks a DBS lending chatbot would answer to, what the DAISY prototype does about each, the evidence in this repo, and what is missing. Sources were checked on 2 November 2026.

This is the team's own reading, not a legal or compliance opinion. "Met in the prototype" means the prototype does this, not that a bank running it would comply.

Our reading: loans and cards are not investment products, so DAISY follows this line by choice. | Investments and insurance route to `out_of_scope`. Listing cards for a stated need (`qa-020`) and checking products against personal details (`b34`) resemble the MAS aggregator example, where a personal shortlist is more likely advice. Legal should rule on both. |

A sidebar caption says conversations are saved on this computer and asks customers to leave out phone numbers and emails. | `app_pages/chat.py` | **Not in the prototype.** The caption doesn't mention staff pages or the model provider. |
| **PDPA, purpose limitation (section 18).** Use data only for appropriate, notified purposes. | The profile holds only the fields the checks need. DAISY doesn't ask for names, contact details or identifiers. | `state.PROFILE_FIELDS`, `engine.REQUIRED_FIELDS`, `prompts.ELIGIBILITY_ASK`, `elig-007` | **Partly.** No written purpose statement. Banker view and Flight recorder show all conversations to anyone using the app. Messages go to the Anthropic API. | `src/recorder.py`, `app.py`, `app_pages/banker.py`, `app_pages/replay.py`, `src/llm.py`, `.gitignore` | **Not in the prototype.** No login, encryption, access control or retention policy. | **[PDPC NRIC guidelines](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/-/media/files/pdpc/pdf-files/advisory-guidelines/advisory-guidelines-for-nric-numbers---310818.pdf) (2018; to be updated, valid meanwhile).** Generally no collecting, using or disclosing NRIC or FIN numbers; treat passport numbers similarly. | **[MAS FEAT](https://www.mas.gov.sg/publications/monographs-or-information-paper/2018/feat) (2018), Fairness, principles 1 to 4.** No unjustified systematic disadvantage, justified personal attributes, review for accuracy and bias. | The engine's personal attributes are age, residency and income, each threshold with a source and quoted evidence. ## What a bank would need to add before going live

- Legal and compliance sign-off, including a ruling on shortlists and needs-based lists.
- A consent and purpose notice, and the customer's agreement before a banker summary is prepared.
- Login, separate staff pages, encrypted logs, access control, a retention and deletion policy, and redaction of phone numbers, emails, passport numbers and spaced NRIC forms.
- A transfer and vendor assessment for the model provider, read against PDPC's [Advisory Guidelines on Use of Personal Data in Generative AI](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/organisations/regulations-decisions/regulatory-guidance/advisory-guidelines-on-use-of-personal-data-in-generative-ai) (issued 20 July 2026, not mapped here).
- A real banker queue with an owner, distress handling that reaches a person, and a complaints route that names FIDReC.
- Formal fairness testing across customer groups, the eval suite in CI with agreed thresholds, and live monitoring.
- Independent validation by people outside the build, a compliance reviewer outside the drafter's model family, a risk rating, board reporting and DBS's own PURE review.
- Engine changes checked by credit policy: each package's thereafter rate, guaranteed loans, joint borrowers, valuation, and the actual Notice 635 tests using bureau data.
